She asked Jesus to make her sister help. He didn’t.

When Jesus came to Martha’s village, she welcomed Him into her home, and then she was pulled in every direction by all the serving. Her sister Mary sat at His feet and listened. Martha asked Jesus to tell Mary to help. He answered, “Martha, Martha, you are anxious and troubled about many things, but one thing is needed” (Luke 10:41–42). He said her name twice and named the worry under all that work. A Bible story about stress and doing it all, from Luke 10:38–42.

Later, when their brother Lazarus died, Martha was the one who went out to meet Jesus on the road (John 11:20). And the Gospel of John says it plainly, in the verse below.

“Now Jesus loved Martha, and her sister, and Lazarus.”
