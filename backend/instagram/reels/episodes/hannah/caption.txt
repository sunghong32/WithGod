She prayed so hard, the priest thought she was drunk.

Hannah had waited years for a child, and every year at the feast her husband’s other wife mocked her for it. At the temple in Shiloh she prayed without a sound, only her lips moving, and told the priest Eli she was pouring out her soul before the LORD. Then she went her way and ate, and her face was no longer sad. Nothing had changed yet. Her son Samuel came later. The whole story is in 1 Samuel 1.
